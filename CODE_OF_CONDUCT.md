# Code of Conduct

We want Sandpost to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and to public spaces when representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation for behavior that breaks this code.

Report problems to the maintainers through a private channel. A dedicated contact address will be published here once the project domain is secured (backlog item 0.1). Reports are handled confidentially.

This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
